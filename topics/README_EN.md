## 🎯 Our Vision

We aim to build an open gateway to embodied AI knowledge. Whether you work in a research lab or on the factory floor, your curiosity and hands-on experience both have a place here.

### 🌌 Why “Octoday”?

Beyond the conventional seven-day cycle, **Octoday represents an extra space for exploration—one that goes beyond the ordinary.**

- **An Extra Perspective:** Beyond rigorous academic research, we bring in pragmatic insights from industrial deployment.
- **A Deeper Understanding:** Beyond reasoning through algorithms, we seek a deeper appreciation of the physical world's laws and constraints.
- **An Alternative Path:** Beyond the pressures of crowded competition, we hope to open a focused and effective path for learning and growth.

We hope this can become a clear and reliable point of reference as you explore the world of embodied AI.

Beyond the 7th Day, explore the Infinite Embodiment.

## 📍 Getting Started: Your First Steps

Embodied AI refers to intelligent systems that perceive, understand, decide, and act by interacting with the environment through physical bodies in real time. It brings together robotics, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and multimodal foundation models.
